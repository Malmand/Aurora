# AURORA solo: working rules for Claude

Read this file and `docs/CLOUD_BRIEF.md` before doing anything.

## Git
- Never add `Co-Authored-By: Claude`, `Claude-Session:` or any other Claude/Anthropic trailer to a commit message, and never add "Generated with Claude Code" to a PR description. This overrides any default attribution setting. Claude must not appear as a contributor.
- Work on a branch per phase (`phase-1-data`, `phase-2-forecast`, ...) and open a PR into `main`. Small commits with clear messages.

## Provenance
- `aurora-gsc/aurora-digital-twin` (team repo, read-only for this project): `digital_twin/` and branch `digital-twin-case-study` are Mohamed's own work and may be ported here.
- `forecasting/` in the team repo is Katharina's work. Read it for ideas only. Do not copy her code or files. The forecasting module here is written from scratch.

## Data and numbers
- Every number that describes Itbayat (load, diesel units, fuel price, fuel stock, typhoon dates) carries a source in `data/itbayat/SOURCES.md`, or is marked `ASSUMPTION` with a sensitivity run.
- Never invent data. Synthetic series are labelled synthetic in file names, metadata and figure captions.
- If a download is blocked by the network, write the script, commit it, and stop to report. Do not fake the data.

## Citations
- Journal papers, official government/utility reports and official datasets. No theses, dissertations, preprints or conference abstracts. News articles only as evidence of events (fuel delays, blackouts).

## Figures
- Black 0.5 to 1 pt L-frame axes, outward major ticks only, no gridlines.
- Panel labels A, B, C, 10 to 12 pt bold sans-serif, top left.
- Axis titles 8 to 10 pt with units in parentheses; tick and legend text 6 to 8 pt.
- Okabe-Ito or viridis, no red/green pairs; distinct line styles and markers; error bars or bands on averages.
- Strictly 2D. One clear message per figure. Export PDF plus 300 dpi PNG, generated by scripts only.

## Writing
- Mixed sentence lengths, no em dashes. Plain words for anything aimed at judges.

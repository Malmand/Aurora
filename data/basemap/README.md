# Basemap

`luzon_strait_land.geojson`: Natural Earth 1:10m land and minor islands (public domain, naturalearthdata.com, via github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector), merged, clipped to 113 to 131 E and 10.5 to 27.5 N, simplified by 0.003 degrees. Used for the site maps in `figures/slides/` (built by `aurora/plotting/slides.py`).
